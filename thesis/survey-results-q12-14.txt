# Survey Results — Questions 12–14 (230 respondents)
# Aligned with Q15 feature preferences and overall platform attitudes

QUESTION 12: Would uploading photos of items help verification?
  - Yes: 77.8% (179 responses)
  - No: 7.8% (18 responses)
  - Not sure: 14.3% (33 responses)

QUESTION 13: Would searchable online listings with filters (category, building, date) help?
  - Yes: 81.3% (187 responses)
  - No: 6.1% (14 responses)
  - Not sure: 12.6% (29 responses)

QUESTION 14: Would a digital claim workflow (submit proof → staff review → notification) be useful?
  - Yes: 79.6% (183 responses)
  - No: 7.0% (16 responses)
  - Not sure: 13.4% (31 responses)
